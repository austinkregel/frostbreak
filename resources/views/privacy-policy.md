# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 16, 2025**

## Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our marketplace and related services.

## What Information We Collect

- **GitHub Authentication:** If you sign in with GitHub, we store your GitHub user ID and a token to access your repositories. We index and store the list of repositories you own or have access to, in order to provide package claiming and management features.
- **Cookies:** We use cookies for authentication and session management. These cookies are required for you to log in and use the site securely.
- **Packages and Repositories:** We repackage repositories from packagist.org to make them compatible with WinterCMS. During this process, we may install composer dependencies to ensure packages work as expected for end users.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide authentication and access to your repositories.
- To allow you to claim, manage, and publish packages on the marketplace.
- To ensure packages are compatible with WinterCMS by repackaging and installing dependencies.

## What We Do **Not** Do

- We do **not** track your activity for advertising or analytics purposes.
- We do **not** and will **never** sell your data to third parties.

## Subscription Fees

We reserve the right to charge subscription fees for access to private repositories or other premium features on the marketplace. Any such changes will be communicated in advance.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support channels provided on the site.
